Privacy policy

JDS Logistics, Navi Mumbai · Last updated 18 October 2026

This policy covers the JDS Logistics operations portal and the JDS WhatsApp bot. Both are internal tools for JDS Logistics staff only. They are not offered to the public.

What the WhatsApp bot does

The bot sends JDS staff a daily summary and status alerts about shipments JDS is handling, and answers staff questions about a job, container or bill of entry. It only talks to mobile numbers that JDS has added to its own list; it ignores messages from any other number.

What information is used

  • The staff member's WhatsApp number and name, to know who is writing.
  • The text of messages staff send to the bot (for example a job number), to work out the answer.
  • Shipment records already kept in the JDS portal (job numbers, container numbers, customs and delivery status).

How it is kept

Information is stored in the JDS portal's database. The bot keeps a short record of which updates it has already sent, so it does not repeat them, and the time a staff member last wrote to it. Messages are not used for advertising, are not sold, and are not shared with anyone outside JDS Logistics, apart from Meta (WhatsApp), which carries the messages.

Removing your information

Staff can ask JDS Logistics at any time to be removed from the bot. Their number is then taken off the list and the bot's records for that number are deleted.
